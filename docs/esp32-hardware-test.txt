Ambi4Tizen – hardware prerequisites and bounded acceptance test

No wiring or power rating may be inferred from the software defaults.
Keep output_enabled=false until the actual hardware has been checked.

Check before connecting a strip:
- Actual LED model/markings and required supply voltage.
- Suitable supply model/rating, wire gauge, fuse and distribution.
- Appropriate data level shifting and its own voltage requirements.
- Exact ESP board, available output GPIO and flash size.
- Shared ground and correct strip data direction.
- Actual number of LEDs on the left, top and right sections.

If the ESP is USB-powered, do not tie its USB 5-V output to an independent
LED power supply output. Verify the actual wiring and common ground rather
than assuming a generic board pinout. Software current estimation is not
electrical protection and does not replace a suitable fuse or power supply.
Do not improvise tests when required safety components are missing.

Suggested acceptance sequence, on hardware you control:
1. Complete private configuration, validate pin/layout/supply and choose a
   new profile_id when replacing an old physical/network configuration.
2. First flash with LED output disabled. Verify Wi-Fi and authenticated status.
   Confirm exact board/flash capacity before choosing flash parameters.
3. Power off before connecting the verified LED setup. Begin with a small,
   dim pattern and checked current budget, never an initial full-white load.
4. Check color order, direction, start, each section and both top corners.
5. Check immediate OFF, stream timeout, manual AMBIENT priority and AUTO fallback.
6. Check reconnect and persistence after restart without changing router settings.
7. Only then install/configure the TV sender and run one bounded 30-second
   test, initially 480x270, 12 zones and 1000-ms target interval.
8. Confirm playback remains normal; inspect timings, memory and receive counts.
   Stop at playback disruption, cleanup failure or repeated errors.

Installation is distinct from active capture. A sender built with enabled=false
does not capture or open its diagnostics port. Configuration changes require
rebuilding and reinstalling the TV package. No boot startup is configured.

Longer runs, higher rates, smaller captures and application switching are
separate validation steps after the first end-to-end success. Hue/Zigbee and
autostart investigation follow later. No root, protected system changes or
firmware/security workarounds are part of this procedure.
